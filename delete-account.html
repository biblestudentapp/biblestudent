Delete Your BibleStudent Account

BibleStudent allows you to permanently delete your account and associated BibleStudent data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once your account has been deleted, your BibleStudent account and associated data cannot be restored.

Delete Your Account in the BibleStudent App

The easiest way to delete your account is directly from BibleStudent.

  1. Open BibleStudent and sign in to your account.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the account deletion warning.
  5. Type DELETE when prompted.
  6. Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account.

What Is Deleted?

When your BibleStudent account is permanently deleted, information associated with your account is deleted through BibleStudent's account deletion process.

This includes BibleStudent data such as:

  • Your BibleStudent account.
  • Your profile information.
  • Your Bible study progress.
  • Your completed challenge history.
  • Your XP and streak information.
  • Your badges and achievements.
  • Your saved verses and favorites.
  • Your saved reflections.

Request Account Deletion Without the App

If you cannot access the BibleStudent app or your account, you may request account deletion by contacting BibleStudent Support.

Send your request from the email address associated with your BibleStudent account whenever possible. We may need to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Request Account Deletion

You may also email biblestudentapp@gmail.com .

Questions?
