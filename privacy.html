Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

BibleStudent respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use BibleStudent, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use a BibleStudent account, we may collect information necessary to provide the app and save your Bible study journey.

This may include:

  • Your email address.
  • Your display name.
  • Your BibleStudent account information.
  • Your Bible study progress.
  • Completed daily challenges.
  • XP, streaks, achievements, and badges.
  • Verses you save or mark as favorites.
  • Personal reflections you choose to save.
  • Whether you indicate that you shared a verse.
  • Your BibleStudent journey start date.

2. How We Use Your Information

BibleStudent uses this information to provide and maintain your personal Bible study experience.

Your information may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Save your Bible study progress.
  • Track completed challenges, XP, streaks, and badges.
  • Display your personal 52-week journey.
  • Save verses, favorites, and reflections.
  • Provide app functionality and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support or account-related requests.

3. Authentication and Data Storage

BibleStudent uses Supabase for account authentication and database services. Your account and BibleStudent data may be processed and stored using Supabase infrastructure.

BibleStudent uses security controls designed to restrict users to information associated with their own accounts.

4. Bible and Scripture Services

BibleStudent may retrieve Bible verse text from external Bible services when you request or view certain Scripture content. These services may process technical information necessary to respond to the request.

5. Notifications

If you choose to enable daily BibleStudent reminders, the app may schedule notifications on your device. You can change your notification permissions through your device settings.

6. Support Purchases

BibleStudent may offer optional ways to support the app in the future. If purchases become available, payments will be processed through the applicable app store payment system. BibleStudent does not directly collect your complete payment card information.

7. Sharing Your Information

BibleStudent does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the app, such as authentication, database, hosting, and app-store services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of BibleStudent and its users.

8. Your Reflections and Saved Content

Reflections, saved verses, favorites, and similar BibleStudent content are associated with your account so that they can be available throughout your BibleStudent journey.

You should avoid entering highly sensitive personal information into your reflections.

9. Account and Data Deletion

BibleStudent provides an account deletion option within the app. When you permanently delete your account, BibleStudent deletes your account and associated BibleStudent data in accordance with the app's account deletion process.

You can also review our Delete Account page for additional information.

10. Data Retention

BibleStudent retains account information and BibleStudent progress while your account remains active or as necessary to provide the service. Information may also be retained when required for legitimate security, legal, or operational purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

BibleStudent is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect BibleStudent information. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as BibleStudent changes. When material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

14. Contact Us
